Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | 1931 - 2015

About

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist, teacher, and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India's missile and space programs, earning the title "Missile Man of India." He inspired millions of students with his humility, dedication, and vision for a developed India.

Major Achievements

  • 🚀 Led India's Missile Development Program.
  • 🛰️ Contributed significantly to ISRO and DRDO.
  • 🇮🇳 Served as the 11th President of India (2002–2007).
  • 🏆 Received India's highest civilian award, the Bharat Ratna.
  • 📚 Authored inspiring books like Wings of Fire.
  • 🎓 Dedicated his life to inspiring students and young innovators.
